How it works

Families post. Caregivers reply.

A family describes the care they need, for free. Verified caregivers and nurses in that region read the request and answer directly. Nobody takes a referral fee or a percentage of anyone's rate.

Who pays what

Caregivers and agencies pay a flat monthly membership for each New York region they work in. Families never pay.

  • Families: free, always
  • Caregivers & nurses: $8.99 / month per region, after a 14-day trial
  • Agencies & facilities: $49.99 / month per region

Privacy

Messaging only. On purpose.

Karing4Life doesn't publish phone numbers or home addresses, not for families and not for caregivers. For a family arranging care for a loved one, that matters.

Locked until a region is unlocked

A caregiver who hasn't unlocked a region sees only that a request exists there, never the details.

Agencies don't see families at all

Agency accounts can post jobs and message caregivers, but family requests never appear for them.

You decide what to share

Every first conversation happens in private messages on the site. You choose when to share contact details, and with whom.

Karing4Life is not a home care agency. We don't employ, place, supervise or dispatch caregivers, and we don't give medical advice. Families and caregivers agree on terms directly with each other.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still unsure? Write to info@karing4life.com and a person replies within one business day.

Do families ever pay?
No. Posting, replying and messaging are free. The only optional cost is $8.99 if you want to contact 10 more caregivers on a single post, beyond the first 10.
Can a caregiver see my request before paying?
They can see that a request exists in your region, and the type of care, but the title and description stay blurred until they unlock that region.
What's the difference between Available and Fill-in?
Available caregivers are open for regular or ongoing work. Fill-in caregivers can still be messaged, but they're only looking for single shifts or a few days, which is ideal when your regular caregiver is away.
How does billing work for several regions?
Each region is $8.99 a month for caregivers ($49.99 for agencies). Add as many as you like and they're billed together in one monthly charge. Cancel anytime.
Does Karing4Life employ or place caregivers?
No. We're a directory. We verify accounts and provide private messaging. Families and caregivers agree on rates, schedules and terms directly.

    FAQ

    Family questions.

    Is it really free?
    Yes. Posting, reading replies and messaging are free for families. The only optional extra is $8.99 to contact 10 more caregivers on a single post.
    Who can see my request?
    Only verified caregivers and nurses who have unlocked your region can read the details. Agencies and facilities can't see family requests at all.
    Why do you ask for the address where care is needed?
    So we can place your request in the right region. The address is never shown to caregivers or anyone else; they see only the town and region.
    Do you check caregivers' backgrounds?
    We verify licenses for HHAs, CNAs, LPNs and RNs, and government ID for non-skilled caregivers. We're a directory, so we encourage families to interview, check references and agree on terms in writing.
