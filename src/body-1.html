Trust & safety

Built on trust from the first message.

A family arranging care for a parent or child is sharing something personal. Every rule here exists to protect that.

Read the full rules

Verified before they reply

Licensed caregivers upload their HHA, CNA, LPN or RN license. Everyone else verifies with a government ID. Each one is reviewed by hand.

Private messaging only

No phone numbers or home addresses are published. The first conversation always happens on the site, and you choose when to share more.

Limits that stop spam

Families post once every 2 weeks and contact 10 caregivers per post, so inboxes stay calm and every message is a real one.

Agencies can't see families

Agency accounts recruit caregivers only. Family care requests are hidden from them entirely, so no recruiter can cold-call a family.
